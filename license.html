License

One key for every way you convert.

A Glimmerglass license is $8, one payment, and yours for life. It unlocks converting your own transparent animations into .glim files, wherever you start.

What it unlocks

  • The Framer plugin. Free to install from the Framer Marketplace; the key turns on converting.
  • ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants. Give the Glimmerglass connector your animation and your key, and it hands back the .glim, its poster and the code for your site. See how to connect an assistant.
  • Playback settings. Play always, on hover or in view, speed, looping and the rest are saved inside the .glim, so they travel with the file.

Using your key with an assistant

When an assistant asks for it, paste your key into the chat once. It is sent to the conversion service to check it and is never written into the files you get. Coding agents can be set up with the key instead, so you never paste it at all.

Your license is for you, on your own browsers, computers and assistants. An assistant counts as one browser.

Getting a key

Keys are sold through Gumroad, which emails the key to you. The Apple app gives App Store buyers their own key. Buy a key, or read license help for finding a lost key, using another computer, and limits.
