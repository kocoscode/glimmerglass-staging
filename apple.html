Make your first .glim.

The Apple app converts on your device. Your animations stay with you, in the app and in your own iCloud Drive, and converting works without an internet connection.

  1. Bring in your animation.Tap + (or Files on iPad) to choose a video, an animated PNG, WebP or GIF, a folder of PNG or WebP frames, a .zip of frames, or a .glim. In your animation app you can also tap Share and choose Glimmerglass. The sample, Gatocat, is already in your library to try.
  2. Check the preview.Open an animation and it shows big in a square. Library is at the top left, and Edit and Export are at the top right. Under the square, the play bar shows the time, and its Zoom button (four corner marks) shows the animation to fit, or each size at 100%.
  3. Choose the settings.Tap Edit. Animation, Playback, Output and Poster each hold a few settings, explained below. Changes save as you go.
  4. Export.The app makes one .glim: every size you ticked, in both video formats, with a still poster. Frames that repeat are only encoded once and keep their timing.
  5. Use it.When it’s ready, send it to Framer, save the .glim, or use Send… to make a see-through video for Keynote, a chroma-key video for TikTok, CapCut or Shorts, or a Telegram sticker.

The editor

Tap Edit to open four sections: Animation, Playback, Output and Poster. On iPad the sections are a rail down the left, and the settings of the open section are round buttons down the right. Each button opens a small card over the preview, one setting at a time. On iPhone the sections are a bar at the bottom, the settings are a row of round buttons above it, and the choices sit just above those.

Changes save as you make them. Close says “Changes saved”. On iPhone and iPad, a .glim you bring in has Animation and Playback only, because its sizes and poster are already made.

On a Mac the editor keeps its own layout: the library in a sidebar, the preview in the middle and a column of settings beside it, with the name, frame rate, quality, sizes, alt text and loop. Playback and Poster are in the iPhone and iPad editor.

Animation

  • Name: names the .glim and the Framer component.
  • Alt text: describes the animation for screen readers and search engines.

Playback

How the animation plays on a page. These settings are saved inside the .glim. In Framer, you set them on the component in Framer’s properties panel instead.

  • Play: Always, On hover or In view.
  • Hover Area: Artwork, or Container, which includes the see-through space around the artwork. It only appears when Play is On hover.
  • Fit: Contain, Cover or Fill, for how it fills the space it’s placed in.
  • Speed: from 0.25× to 4×.
  • Loop: On, or Off to play once and stop on the last frame.
  • Pausable: visitors can click or tap it to pause and play it again.
  • Play bar: Hidden, or Shown as a play button and timeline under the animation.
  • Background: the colour behind the animation. None keeps it see-through, or choose White, Black, Green, Magenta or Blue. The preview shows it.

Output

These shape the videos, so they take effect when you export.

  • Frame rate: taken from the file. Frames with no timing of their own need one, so you choose it.
  • Quality: a choice between smaller files and more detail.
  • Sizes: tick the sizes to make. At least one stays ticked. Each shows its measured size, and the figures update as you change Quality or Frame rate.

The play bar shows the size of the whole .glim. A visitor only ever loads one video from it, the one that suits their screen and browser.

Poster

The poster is the still picture a page shows before the animation plays, and where a browser can’t play it. Drag along the filmstrip under the preview to the frame you want, then tap Make Poster. Automatic chooses one for you.

Put it in Framer

Your App Store purchase includes a key for the Glimmerglass plugin in Framer. In the app, open Settings › Framer plugin key and tap Get my key, then paste it into the plugin when it asks. Send the .glim to your computer with AirDrop or Files and drop it into the plugin: it places the animation without converting it again.

Use it elsewhere

A .glim is a single file holding every size and both video formats; here is what’s inside. The Framer plugin reads it directly. For apps that don’t know .glim, tap Send… and choose what to make. Slides & video makes a see-through .mov for Keynote, Final Cut Pro and iMovie; Chroma-key video puts it on green, magenta or blue; Sticker makes a Telegram sticker.

Artwork that works well

Start with a transparent MOV (including HEVC with alpha or ProRes 4444), WebM, animated PNG, animated WebP, GIF, or equally sized PNG or WebP frames. A file ending in .mp4 does not necessarily contain transparency.

Glimmerglass keeps transparency; it does not cut out a background. If an animation has none, the app says so and asks whether to keep its background.

Animations can be up to 120 seconds, 7,200 frames and 16 megapixels a frame. Long animations and large frames need more memory and free space, so start with a short clip.

Common questions

Where is my library?

In the app, and in your own iCloud Drive when it’s turned on, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac show the same animations. Nothing is stored on our servers. A deleted animation stays in the app’s trash for 30 days.

Why is the finished .glim a different size from the estimate?

Estimates come from a short sample and are rounded up, so the finished file is usually a little smaller. Long animations whose motion changes a lot can differ more.

What should I put in the alt text?

Describe it for screen readers, and creepy crawlers.

Can I change the frame rate?

When the file has its own timing, the app keeps it, including held frames. Frames without timing need a frame rate: choose one under Output to set how fast they play.

Will a website use my Playback settings?

Playback settings are saved in the .glim. The Glimmerglass web player is learning to read them; until then a page uses its own settings.

Does the app need the internet?

Only to get your Framer plugin key, once, when you ask for it. See privacy.

Still stuck?

Include your device, operating-system version, source format and the exact error. You don’t need to send your artwork unless we ask for a sample.
