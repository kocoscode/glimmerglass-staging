Your files, explained.

Glimmerglass is made by The House of Koco. The Apple app processes locally. The Framer plugin uses a conversion service. Here is what that means for your files.

Apple app privacy policy

The Apple app converts files on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. It does not send source files, generated media, filenames or descriptions to us.

Files you choose

The app reads files and folders you select and stores completed conversions in its Documents folder. Original files are unchanged. You control any copies you save or share. You can remove local app data using your device’s storage controls; copies you exported elsewhere remain there until you delete them.

Collection and tracking

The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking. Conversion does not require a connection to our servers.

Purchases and sharing

Apple handles App Store purchases. Glimmerglass does not collect payment details. A destination you choose when sharing an export handles that file under its own terms.

Framer plugin data handling

The Framer plugin uploads your animation to our server in Germany over HTTPS for conversion. It does not convert files on your device.

Your selected artwork

Choosing an animation in the plugin uploads its files and their names to the conversion service straight away, so the service can measure them and estimate file sizes. Changing a setting sends the settings, not your files again. Pressing Create Component starts the conversion. A .glim file you already made is read in your browser and not sent to our server.

Your uploaded files are deleted from our server as soon as the conversion finishes or fails. The finished files never get a public download link: only the plugin that started the job can fetch them, with a private code for that job, and they are deleted as soon as it has them. Anything left behind, such as a job you abandoned, is deleted 30 minutes after its upload began. If the converter stops unexpectedly during a job, that cleanup can take longer.

The plugin uploads the finished media to the Framer project you are editing, and Framer keeps it there. The component includes references to those files and its player. Download in the plugin builds its file in your browser, not on our server.

License verification

The plugin stores your key, a random device identifier and the time of the last successful check in that browser’s local storage. It sends the key and device identifier to the conversion service, which checks the key with Gumroad. Your license is for you, on your own browsers and computers. There's a soft limit of 5 browsers to catch shared keys - normal use never touches it. The service checks again before accepting an upload. If a purchase is refunded, its key stops working.

To notice a key being shared widely, the service keeps a one-way fingerprint of the key, which can't be turned back into it, and of each device identifier that used it, with the hour each device was first seen and the time of the last check. Each browser is remembered for 30 days, then quietly re-verified with Gumroad. It does not keep the key itself, your name or your email address. A key that appears on many new devices in a short time, or starts conversions at an unusual rate, may be slowed or paused. Removing the license from a browser clears the key saved there. Keys are not embedded in generated components.

Operational information

The service uses a temporary one-way license identifier for short-lived request limits. Logs record service events and errors. They never contain your files, filenames or full license key.

Server logs may keep Gumroad order references for license support and administration.

The server is backed up once a day, and each backup is kept for 7 days. A backup copies everything on the server at that moment, including the license records above and the files of any job not yet deleted. So deleted data, including your artwork and its finished files, can remain in a backup for up to 7 days.

Purchases and license keys are handled by Gumroad. Project assets are handled by Framer. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive what you include and use it to respond. Include the error, browser and input format. For a purchase question, include your order reference. Do not send your full license key or private artwork.

This support website does not add analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting service may process connection and security information to deliver the site.
